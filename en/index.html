Sorting Express privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy describes Sorting Express (Expreso Clasificador in Spanish), a game for Android.

In short

  • There are no accounts. The game asks for no name, email, birth date, photos, contacts or location.
  • Everything the game keeps stays on the phone. The developer does not receive it.
  • The game does not ask for the player's age.
  • There are Google AdMob ads, and all of them are for children.
  • There are no purchases of any kind.

What the game keeps on the phone

The save. One file with:

  • the stars of each level and the open worlds;
  • the album: which cards were delivered, which tricks were passed and which are mastered;
  • the review boxes, which decide when a missed card comes back;
  • the settings: sound, music, vibration, volumes, no-rush mode, less motion and language;
  • today's best stars in the daily challenge, and the survival mode's record;
  • the days played and the time of the last game;
  • for the last 30 days, how many trains reached their station and how many came back, by day and by topic, shown in the statistics;
  • the speed the game adjusts to on its own;
  • how many ads showed today and when the last one showed, to keep to the daily cap.

None of this says who the player is. Earlier versions kept the age band in a separate file; the game deletes it when it opens.

How long. Everything stays on the phone until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared in Android settings.

Backup

If the phone has Android backup turned on, the save may be kept in the Google account of the person who uses the phone, or moved to a new phone when it is set up. That way the album is not lost with a new phone. Android makes that copy, not the game, and the developer cannot see it. It is kept in the cloud only if the phone has a screen lock, encrypted with it.

Ads

The game is free and is paid for by Google AdMob ads. They show when a game ends, at most one every 3 minutes, never mid-game, and can be closed. There are no reward videos.

  • For children, always. Every ad is requested as child-directed, with content rated G at most, whoever plays.
  • Nothing at the start. The game does not start AdMob, or any other ad service, during the tutorial, which is level 1.
  • No advertising id. The app does not ask for the Android advertising id.

To show ads, Google may process device data, such as the IP address, the phone model or whether an ad was tapped. It does so under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. The developer builds no profiles, sells no data and uses no analytics tools.

Children

The game is made for children aged 6 to 12, and treats every player as a child. It does not check anyone's identity or ask for contact details. A summary of this policy is also inside the game, in the settings, as text.

Sound

The game does not read aloud, records no sound and does not use the microphone.

Changes and contact

The date above changes when this policy changes. For any question, the contact email is on the game's page on Google Play.
